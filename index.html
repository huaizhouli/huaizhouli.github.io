I am an Early Career Academic Fellow in Economics at UNSW Sydney. I received my PhD in Finance from UNSW Business School and was previously a Postdoctoral Researcher in Finance at the Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth College. My research examines corporate finance, entrepreneurial finance, product-market competition, and international trade, often using large-scale textual and alternative data.
